Work life at Toggl

Fully remote since 2014

We believe in working from wherever you can do your best work. That looks different for everyone, which is why we have colleagues from all around the world.

Driven by a purpose

We're guided by a strong mission, a distinct vision, and shared values. Trust, our key value, shapes the company culture and direction.

Work freedom

It doesn't matter when you work or how long you work, as long as you get things done. We call this Results and Accountability First at Toggl, or RAFT™.

Small but impactful

Each Toggler has a significant impact on what their role entails, embracing accountability and ownership in their day-to-day.

Challenging (the good kind)

Our globally distributed and diverse team members are as talented as they are opinionated. You'll work together with the best people — each with their unique skillset and perspective — to tackle real problems.

And... meetups!

As a remote team, we know that virtual calls are not enough and face time is essential. We aim to meet up at least twice a year. And yes, all expenses are covered!

Our hiring process

There might be slight differences based on the position and seniority. For the most accurate information, please visit the individual job posting.

  1. Skills assessmentsA short online skills test for the role. It helps us look beyond resumes and gives everyone a fair chance to show their skills and way of thinking.
  2. AI interview NewA short interview with our AI interviewer, done online whenever suits you. It asks about your experience and motivation, and our Talent team reviews every answer. A person always makes the call.On your scheduleReviewed by humans
  3. Home assignment NewA practical task close to the real work, so you can show how you think. We keep it short and tell you up front how long it should take.
  4. Technical interviewA call with the team lead to share more details about the team you'll be working with while diving deeper into your technical background and experience.
  5. Paid test weekYou will be paid to join us for 3-5 days. Here, you will have a chance to interact with your future team and work on tasks you would normally work on if hired.
  6. OfferWe make an offer to the candidate we feel will thrive with us and is the best addition for the team

Questions candidates ask us New

Still curious? Ask us anything in your application.

Do I need to love time tracking?

No. You need to care about building something people use every day. Most of us learned to love the timer after joining.

Can I work from anywhere?

We're fully remote. Each job post lists the countries and timezones we can hire in for that role.

What's the AI interview like?

A short online interview you do whenever suits you, about your experience and why you're interested. Our Talent team reviews every answer, and a person always makes the decision. Collecting

Is the test week really paid?

Yes. You join the team for 3–5 days, work on real tasks, and we pay you for it.

How long does the whole process take?

Usually a few weeks from application to offer. Each step tells you what comes next. Collecting

Will I hear back if it's a no?

Yes, we reply to every applicant. Collecting
